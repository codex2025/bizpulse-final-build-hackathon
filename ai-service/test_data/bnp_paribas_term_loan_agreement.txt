BNP PARIBAS (INDIA BRANCHES)
SENIOR SECURED COMMERCIAL TERM LOAN AGREEMENT

FACILITY REFERENCE: BNPP/IN/CORP-TL/2024-8802
DATED: 12 September 2024

PARTIES:
1. BNP PARIBAS, acting through its India branches, licensed by the Reserve Bank of India, having its Principal Office at Maker Chambers III, Nariman Point, Mumbai - 400021 (the "Lender").
2. ORION LOGISTICS & WAREHOUSING PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with CIN U60200MH2016PTC288129 (the "Borrower").

ARTICLE I: THE FACILITY
1.1 Facility Amount: The Lender makes available to the Borrower a Senior Secured Term Loan facility in an aggregate principal amount of ₹25,00,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Crores Only).
1.2 Purpose: The facility shall be applied exclusively towards financing the construction of an automated logistics park in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra.
1.3 Upfront Commitment Fee: 1.00% of the facility amount payable on the execution date.

ARTICLE II: INTEREST, BENCHMARK & MARGIN
2.1 Rate of Interest: Floating rate equal to 3-Month Treasury Bill Yield + Margin of 4.25% per annum.
2.2 Repricing & Spreads: Interest is payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September, and December.
2.3 Unilateral Margin Revision: The Lender shall be entitled to review and adjust the applicable Margin annually based on the Borrower's Debt-to-Equity and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR).

ARTICLE III: AMORTIZATION & MANDATORY PREPAYMENT
3.1 Repayment: The facility shall be amortized over a 7-year tenure through 28 equal consecutive quarterly installments of ₹89,28,571/- starting from Q1 2025.
3.2 Mandatory Prepayment from Asset Sales: In the event the Borrower disposes of any real estate or subsidiary capital assets exceeding ₹1,00,00,000/-, 100% of net cash proceeds must be immediately applied towards mandatory prepayment of this facility.
3.3 Voluntary Prepayment: Permitted upon 30 days prior written notice, subject to a Breakage Cost and a Prepayment Penalty of 2.00% of the prepaid principal.

ARTICLE IV: FINANCIAL COVENANTS & NEGATIVE PLEDGE
4.1 The Borrower shall maintain throughout the tenure:
  (a) Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) not less than 1.35x.
  (b) Total Debt to Tangible Net Worth ratio not exceeding 2.50x.
4.2 Negative Pledge: The Borrower shall not create or permit to subsist any mortgage, charge, hypothecation, pledge, or encumbrance over any of its present or future revenues or assets without prior written consent of the Lender.
4.3 Subordination of Shareholder Debt: All loans from directors and shareholders shall remain junior and subordinate to the Lender's claims.

ARTICLE V: EVENTS OF DEFAULT & CROSS-DEFAULT
5.1 Events of Default include:
  (a) Non-payment of principal, interest, or fees continuing beyond 3 Business Days.
  (b) Cross-Default: Any default or acceleration under any indebtedness of the Borrower or its affiliates exceeding ₹50,00,000/- with any bank or financial institution.
  (c) Material Adverse Change in financial condition or operational viability.
5.2 Remedies on Default: Upon default, the Lender may:
  (a) Cancel the facility and declare all outstanding loans, accrued interest, and fees immediately due and payable.
  (b) Enforce the hypothecation and mortgage charges under applicable debt recovery tribunals and SARFAESI mechanisms.
  (c) Apply sweeping Right of Set-Off against any balances held with the Lender or its global affiliates.

ARTICLE VI: GOVERNING LAW & ARBITRATION
6.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
6.2 Any dispute arising hereunder shall be finally settled under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Lender, with seat of arbitration in Mumbai.
